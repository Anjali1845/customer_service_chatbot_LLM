AURORA BREEZE SPEAKER - TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE (VERSION 1.0)

THE SPEAKER WILL NOT TURN ON

1. Charge the speaker for thirty minutes using the USB-C cable.
2. Press and hold the power button for three seconds.
3. If the status light stays off after charging, contact Aurora support.

THE SPEAKER WILL NOT CONNECT OVER BLUETOOTH

1. Move the speaker within ten metres of your phone.
2. Turn Bluetooth off and on again on your phone.
3. Press and hold the Bluetooth button until the status light flashes, then select "Aurora Breeze" again.
4. If the speaker still will not connect, reset the speaker.

SOUND IS DISTORTED OR TOO QUIET

1. Check that the volume on your phone is above fifty percent.
2. Clean the speaker grille with a dry cloth.
3. Turn the speaker off for one minute, then turn it on again.

PLAYBACK STOPS AFTER A FEW MINUTES

1. Charge the speaker for two hours. A low battery can interrupt playback.
2. Move the speaker away from microwaves and routers.
3. If playback still stops, reset the speaker and pair the phone again.

FACTORY RESET

Press and hold the reset button for ten seconds. The status light flashes three times and the speaker restarts with factory settings. After a reset you must pair your phone again. If problems continue after a reset, contact Aurora support.
