AURORA BREEZE SPEAKER - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS, EU EDITION (VERSION 1.0)

Questions from EU customers about the Aurora Breeze speaker.

Q: How long does the battery last?
A: A fully charged Aurora Breeze speaker plays for up to twelve hours at medium volume.

Q: How do I pair the speaker with a second phone?
A: Hold the Bluetooth button for five seconds, then select "Aurora Breeze" on the new phone. The speaker stores up to eight paired devices.

Q: Can I use the speaker while it charges?
A: Yes. The speaker continues to play while it charges over USB-C.

Q: Is the speaker waterproof?
A: No. The speaker is water resistant, so it can handle light rain, but it must not be submerged.

Q: How do I reset the speaker?
A: Press and hold the reset button for ten seconds. The status light flashes three times and the speaker restarts with factory settings.

Q: What does the warranty cover in the EU?
A: The warranty covers manufacturing defects for 24 months from the purchase date, in line with EU consumer law. It does not cover water damage or accidental drops.

Q: Where can I find the firmware version?
A: Open the Aurora companion app, choose your speaker, and select "About". The firmware version appears on that screen.

Q: Can I return the speaker?
A: Contact the store where you bought the speaker. Returns follow the store's own return terms; the Aurora Breeze warranty covers manufacturing defects only.
