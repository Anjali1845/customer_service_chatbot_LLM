AURORA BREEZE SPEAKER - USER GUIDE (VERSION 2.0)

Version 2.0 of the Aurora Breeze speaker user guide ships with the second generation Aurora Breeze speaker.

WHAT IS NEW IN VERSION 2.0

- Stereo pairing with a second Aurora Breeze speaker
- Twelve hour battery life
- Updated companion app with firmware updates

GETTING STARTED

1. Charge the speaker for two hours using the USB-C cable.
2. Press and hold the power button for three seconds until the status light turns blue.
3. Open the Aurora companion app to finish setup.

WARRANTY

The second generation Aurora Breeze speaker includes a 24 month manufacturer warranty from the date of purchase. The warranty covers manufacturing defects.
