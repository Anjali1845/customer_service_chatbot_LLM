AURORA BREEZE SPEAKER - USER GUIDE (VERSION 1.0)

Thank you for purchasing the Aurora Breeze portable Bluetooth speaker.

WHAT IS IN THE BOX

- Aurora Breeze speaker
- USB-C charging cable
- Quick-start leaflet

GETTING STARTED

1. Charge the speaker for two hours using the USB-C cable.
2. Press and hold the power button for three seconds until the status light turns blue.
3. The speaker is now ready to pair with your phone or tablet.

PAIRING WITH A PHONE OR TABLET

1. Enable Bluetooth on your phone or tablet.
2. Press and hold the Bluetooth button until the status light flashes.
3. Select "Aurora Breeze" from the list of available devices.
4. The speaker announces "connected" when pairing is complete.

The Aurora Breeze speaker remembers up to eight paired devices.

BATTERY AND CHARGING

A full charge provides up to twelve hours of playback. When the battery is low, the status light turns red and the speaker plays a warning tone. The speaker continues to play while it charges.

HOW TO RESET THE SPEAKER

Press and hold the reset button for ten seconds. The status light flashes three times and the speaker restarts with factory settings. After a reset you must pair your phone again.

WARRANTY

The Aurora Breeze speaker includes a 24 month manufacturer warranty from the date of purchase. The warranty covers manufacturing defects. It does not cover water damage or accidental drops.

CARE

Clean the speaker with a dry cloth. The speaker is water resistant, not waterproof. Do not submerge the speaker in water.
