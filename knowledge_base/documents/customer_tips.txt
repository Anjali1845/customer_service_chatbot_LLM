COMMUNITY TIPS FOR THE AURORA BREEZE SPEAKER (VERSION 1.0)

These tips were submitted by customers in the community forum.

TIP 1 - SAFE VOLUME

Keep the volume below seventy percent for comfortable listening and to make the battery last longer.

TIP 2 - PAIRING ORDER

Pair the speaker before you open your music app, so the app sends sound to the right device.

TIP 3 - LABELLED CARRY CASE

A labelled carry case helps when several people bring the same speaker to a meeting.
